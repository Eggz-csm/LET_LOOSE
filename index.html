About The Project

10/30/2025 | A game development project by Gabriel Farley, Ewan Carver, and Grace Perry.

Working a simple job in a classified center, one day you see alarms flash, hear screams tear through the halls, then absolute silence. At that moment, you know it has been let loose.

LET LOOSE is a metroidvania-shooter with horror elements, inspired by Dead Cells and CARRION. The game features multiple guns, enemies, levels, story progression, and a possible infinite mode. All lore, weapons, and environments are subject to change during development.
